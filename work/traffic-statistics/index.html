Traffic statistics

From a road camera, count every vehicle lane by lane, tell what kind it is, and measure its speed, its length and the gap to the one ahead. I wrote this module, from its first measure to the zone that places itself.

The idea, drawn

Problem

Seeing a vehicle is not counting it.

One vehicle can be seen twice, a track can break in the middle, the box around a car says little about its true length, and a counting line in the wrong place misses vehicles.

Goal

Count as well as a person counting by hand.

On any camera, without anyone tuning it by hand.

My part

I wrote the module, start to finish.

Every measure in it, its tests, and the tools to judge it against hand counts. Since 2026 I work with an AI agent: I set the rules and give every verdict; the agent reads the code and writes tests and changes under my direction.

The path

  1. 2024

    From a crossing to measures

    A vehicle crossing the line has to become a count, a class, a speed, a time spent in the zone and a gap to the vehicle ahead.

    Decided: time the crossing between two frames rather than round it to a frame, and measure the speed only on the points inside the zone.

  2. 2024 – 2025

    One vehicle, counted once

    Replayed against hand counts, the module counted some vehicles twice and lost others whose track broke.

    Decided: a second count less than 200 ms after the first is the same vehicle; a broken track is counted as one; each direction is counted on its own.

  3. Apr 2026

    A box lies about a car's length

    Far from the camera, the box around a car stretches with the road: a 4.5 m car came out about 10 m long at 75 m.

    Decided: correct the error by distance, and let the camera find its own height from the cars it sees.

  4. May – Oct 2026

    A zone that places itself

    The counting line was placed by hand on each camera, and a bad place misses vehicles.

    Decided: learn where vehicles are seen best on each lane, and move the line only when it is off by more than 4 m and 5 % of the lane. A vehicle crossing while the line moves is counted once: nine cases, each tested.

Step 3, to play with

A box lies about a car's length

The camera sees a car only as a box. Read on the road, the box's length grows with distance, because its top edge is the car's roof, not the road. Move the car away, then take its height out.

The cars give the camera its height

Reading a length at roof height needs the camera's height, and a wrong height bends every length. A car is about 1.8 m wide, so the camera's height is the one at which the cars it sees come out that wide.

Where it stands

  • 5.96 → 2.73 mMean length error, on 59 real vehicles.
  • 0.6 ptBetween the self-placing line and a line placed by hand, at no visible cost on 22 live cameras.

Key decision

The self-placing line is not switched on yet

Context
Over 1,730 hand-counted vehicles it nearly matches the hand-placed line, but loses about 2 points on the 3 sequences that count well.
Decision
Ship it off by default, behind a setting, until it is measured on more sequences.
Set aside
Switching it on now; tuning it on 5 sequences only.
Cost
For now, the line is still placed by hand on each camera.
