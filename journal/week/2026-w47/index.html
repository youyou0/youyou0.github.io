Week 47 · 16–22 Nov 2026

The self-placing counting zone gives each vehicle one vote, keeps the vehicles it catches while moving, stays where it was drawn to the pixel when it has no reason to move, and is approved in review; on the site, a case on working with AI agents and a career told like a film.

  1. Mon 16 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    One vehicle, one vote for where the counting zone goes

    The zone that places itself listened to each vehicle’s path in proportion to how many points it had, so a slow vehicle spoke many times over. Now every path gives one vote, shared across the lanes it crosses. A test with many short paths and one long one failed before the change and passes after.

  2. Mon 16 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    A vehicle caught by a zone move is still counted

    When the counting zone moved while a vehicle was inside it, the module started over and that vehicle was lost. It is now counted once, with the zone placing itself or not, and two tests hold it.

  3. Tue 17 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    A tunnel scene counts as it did before

    On a tunnel scene where the self-placing zone had been missing vehicles, the one-vote change brings the count back to the old code’s. With the feature switched off, every lane counts exactly as before, so turning it off changes nothing.

  4. Tue 17 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    The moved zone stays out of the operator’s settings

    A zone that moves by itself lives in memory only: it never replaces the zone the operator drew, which would restart every detector on each move. The rule is written in the code, next to the switch that enables it, with its test. Two days later the guard was made complete: a moved zone now travels in a copy of its own, so nothing is ever written into the zone the operator drew.

  5. Tue 17 Nov
    This site

    Animations for every visitor, and pictures that fit the screen

    The drawings that explain each case now play as soon as the page opens, whatever the visitor’s system settings, and a click still pauses them. The opening picture of each case never runs taller than the screen, and a link to any page now shows its picture when it is shared.

  6. Thu 19 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    A zone with no reason to move stays exactly where it was drawn

    A zone that had not moved was still rebuilt from a distance along the lane, rounded where the drawing is cut off, so it could land one row away and miss a vehicle. It is now the drawn zone itself, to the pixel. A test failed before the fix and passes after.

  7. Thu 19 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    A stopped vehicle no longer pulls the zone

    A vehicle waiting in a queue piled up points in the same place, and the zone drifted towards where it stood. Points that do not move no longer vote; only moving traffic says where the zone goes.

  8. Thu 19 Nov
    This site

    A case on working with AI agents

    How a run is framed, the loop the agent runs inside it, and the steps fixed in advance, with live diagrams and the thread a piece of work follows from the request to the verdict. Every case also gets a plan of its parts, every Journal entry a picture, and the pages got lighter.

  9. Fri 20 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    The self-placing counting zone, approved in review

    Every comment answered, in the code or in the text, and the change approved. On the full set of recorded scenes, no lane counts worse than before, and the limits it does not reach are written down with it.

  10. Fri 20 Nov
    This site

    The Path, told like a film

    The career now plays post by post: a line, a picture or a video, and a mark for every task, with the skills shown over the picture. It pauses and resumes anywhere, the videos have their own controls, and a page under every page says how the site is made and whose pictures and videos it shows.
