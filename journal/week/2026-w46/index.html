  1. Tue 10 Nov
    Way of working

    One contract for every pull request, and a first test of it

    Whoever or whatever wrote the code, a pull request should arrive with the same things: ten criteria, drawn from a year of the team’s review comments. A first automatic check, replayed on 15 past cases, already caught issues reviewers had raised, and showed what information it needs to catch the others.

  2. Thu 12 Nov
    Way of working

    A talk on working with an AI assistant as a team

    The point is not the tool but the missing model: each of us gives it instructions in our own corner, with no contract between us. The talk separates what frames one run, the loop the assistant runs inside it, and the sequence of steps we fix in advance, then places each one in the team’s chain, from the request to the release. Built slide by slide, every claim checked against its sources.
