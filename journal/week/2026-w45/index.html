  1. Mon 2 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    A counting zone move no longer resets the other detectors

    A camera runs a dozen detectors side by side. The counting zone now moves by itself as traffic settles, and that small change used to make every detector start over. Now only the counting takes it; the others carry on with what they were following.

  2. Mon 2 Nov

    streetscore, on GitHub

    A street plays itself: people and vehicles tracked with YOLO pluck invisible strings, and the crowd turns into music and light.

  3. Tue 3 Nov
    Testing

    A clean week of logs, weighed against what the old code would have done

    As the tester of a colleague’s fix: a week on a server with 16 working cameras showed no error, where the old code would have produced about thirty. The counts on 5 recorded scenes did not move.

  4. Tue 3 Nov
    Testing

    A clean week, and how often the bug could have fired

    Another fix, for a rare race between two cameras: no error in a week, but the risky moment happened only once, for 98 seconds. The fix was accepted with that limit written down rather than hidden.

  6. Tue 3 Nov

    A shadow-puppet game in the browser: the webcam reads your hand shapes, and each shape becomes a creature, a bird, a wolf or a snake, that can cross what the others cannot.

  7. Wed 4 Nov
    Way of working

    How a pull request should be written, from the literature

    Books, studies and forty of the team’s recent work items, read side by side. The pull request now says why, what changes, the decisions, the risks, the evidence and how to review it; the work item says the need, when it is done, and what is not in scope. Each fact has one home.

  8. Wed 4 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    The self-placing counting zone, up for review

    Three rounds of code review before the pull request went up as a draft. One find on the way: a distance stored in decimetres was divided as an integer, so a zone set at 14.5 m counted at 14 m. Fixed, with a test that failed before.

  9. Thu 5 Nov
    Traffic statistics

    A regression found before publishing, not after

    The counting autotest on a tunnel scene: with the zone placing itself, 9 vehicles of 229 missed, against 1 before. Not noise, six earlier runs give the same counts, and every new miss comes after the zone reached about 32 m. A run with the zone held there points to its position rather than its moves. The pull request waits until that is settled.
