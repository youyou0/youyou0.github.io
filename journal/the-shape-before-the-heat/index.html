The shape before the heat

When a road's two directions come out on top of each other, one lane has to give way. Not the quieter one, it turned out: the one that bends out of line.

I set the rules; an AI agent proposed the measures and wrote the code with me; the tests came before each change.

What was odd

The two directions' lanes came out on top of each other.

The software learns a camera's lanes from the traffic, one lane for each direction. On a 24-hour motorway sequence, the two lanes overlapped instead of sitting side by side. A vehicle in the shared strip would belong to both directions at once, and an incident there to neither for sure.

What I expected, and what I saw

Nothing ever compared the two finished lanes.

I expected something to keep them apart. Reading the code showed that each lane was built from its own traffic, and that was all.

Why a lane spills over in the first place, we did not find. Two explanations stayed open: vehicles counted in the wrong direction, or the two directions filtered unevenly. Neither was confirmed.

A first answer

A guard, without waiting for the cause: the busier lane keeps the space.

Where the lanes overlap, the one that sees more traffic there keeps the space, and the other is cut back to its edge.

7 / 8test cases failed on the symptom before the change; seven of eight passed after it.

The case that did not fit

On a lane that swings, the busier lane was the wrong one.

The eighth case was a lane swinging sideways over its neighbour in the middle of the picture. There, the swinging lane was the busier one, so the rule cut the straight lane, the one that was right.

The search

Four ideas died first, each for a reason worth keeping.

  • Comparing slopes. The middle of a swing is as straight as the rest of the lane, only shifted, so the slopes match.
  • One straight line through each lane's whole centre. The centre is pushed outward by the hard shoulder added to the lane.
  • A threshold as a share of the image width. It ignored perspective: a metre near the camera is far wider on screen than a metre far away.
  • Cutting again and again until nothing overlaps. On one case it erased a whole lane.

What it was

Real lanes run side by side: the lane whose shape breaks is the faulty one.

So when two lanes claim the same space, the shape decides first, whatever the traffic.

Each lane is now compared with its own line, drawn through its edge where it does not overlap. Its deviation over the shared zone is measured in car widths at that distance, so perspective is accounted for. The lane that deviates by at least one and a half car widths, and twice as much as the other, gives way; when the shape cannot tell, traffic decides, as before.

What changed, and the proof

The swing case now gives the right lane, and nothing else moved.

4 / 4overlapping lane pairs resolved on the 24-hour real sequence (4 of 20 pairs overlapped); no lane removed.
106tests pass; every earlier case keeps its result, and a case with two separate shared zones gives the expected lane too.

What it costs: a lane that overlaps along nearly its whole length has no healthy part to draw its line from, so traffic still decides there.
