Lanes bench

A camera has just turned, and nobody is there to draw its lanes. Four roads where the lanes it learns from the traffic come out wrong, each for a reason I had to solve. Pick a challenge, and fix it.

  1. Watch. Press Start: once 100 cars have passed each way, the lanes are built.
  2. Change. Change one of the algorithm’s choices, at the top of the panel.
  3. Check. The lanes are built again from the same cars, and the bench gives its verdict.
