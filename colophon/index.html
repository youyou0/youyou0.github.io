About this site

How it is made, and where what it shows comes from.

Made with

Built with Astro as plain pages: HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, no framework running in the browser. The figure on the home page, the diagrams and the animations are SVG and canvas, drawn for this site. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

I built it with an AI agent, the way I work at my job; how that goes is told in Working with AI agents.

Type

Cormorant Garamond for titles, IBM Plex Sans for text and IBM Plex Mono for labels, served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

Icons and logos

The logos of tools on the Path come from Simple Icons (CC0); the other icons are drawn for this site. The logos of companies and schools belong to them.

Pictures

Pictures from Citilog and Forssea Robotics are theirs. The scenes from the Lab, the charts from the Journal and the drawings on the Path are made for this site. Elsewhere, a picture that is not mine is credited where it appears.

The Path also shows a free picture from Wikimedia Commons:

  • Melanoma in situ on dermoscopy, by Braga et al., CC BY 4.0

Videos

The loop of lanes on the Path is recorded from this site’s own animation. The other videos are their owners’ own, played in their host’s player: YouTube’s in its privacy-enhanced mode, Vimeo’s without tracking; a video loads only when it is shown.

  • Lidar point cloud segmentation, by Deepen AI
  • Shelf monitoring at Auchan, by Neurolabs
  • Traffic statistics and vulnerable users, by Citilog
  • Wrong-way driving detection with Citilog, by Sensys Networks
  • Citilog presence detection at a junction, by CamCentral Systems
  • Atoll docking on visual markers, and Argos sea trials, by Forssea Robotics

Visits

Visits are counted by GoatCounter: no cookies, nothing that follows you from one site to another, only which pages are read and how often. The YouTube player, when a video shows, is YouTube’s own and follows its rules.
